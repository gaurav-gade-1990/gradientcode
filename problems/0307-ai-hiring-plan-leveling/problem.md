### Context

You are the Director of AI Engineering at a K-12 education SaaS company that sells a learning platform to school districts: $210M ARR, 1,900 district customers, 6.5M students on the platform. The board has approved a new AI team of 12 to build two products: an AI tutor that gives students step-by-step hints inside their assignments, and help-desk triage for district and school staff. The AI tutor is a paid add-on. Districts buy for the school year, so sales forecasts $6M of new ARR in its first year if it ships in the back-to-school release, 6 months from now. You present the hiring plan and the leveling approach to the exec staff in 2 weeks.

### What you have

- **Budget.** 12 heads and $3.6M a year of fully loaded run-rate cost. The CFO has said the $3.6M won't move.
- **The VP Product's ask.** 2 staff engineers (L6), 6 senior (L5) and 4 mid-level (L4).
- **Company bands**, fully loaded per year (salary, bonus, equity, benefits; assumed figures from HR): L4 $230k, L5 $290k, L6 $370k.
- **Market for AI engineers**, same basis (comp survey, assumed): L4 $260k, L5 $350k, L6 $460k.
- **Offer history.** In the last 6 months the company made 11 AI offers at company bands and 3 were accepted. 5 of the 8 who declined cited a competing offer one level higher. Engineering offers where the band matches the market are accepted about 70% of the time.
- **Recruiting.** One technical recruiter, who can make about 4 offers a month (assumed). First offers go out about 6 weeks after a role opens, and hires start about a month after accepting.
- **Internal transfers.** 9 engineers applied to an AI transfer track and 4 passed the same interview loop external candidates take: 2 at L5 and 2 at L4. A transfer can start in 30 days, and engineering has agreed to backfill each one from its own approved plan.
- **Existing engineers.** Product engineering has 40 L5 engineers, all paid within the standard L5 band.
- **The work.** Product estimates the AI tutor needs 5 engineers, help-desk triage 4, and shared evaluation and platform work 3. Help-desk triage has no revenue attached; it is scoped at 24 engineer-months and aims to cut support tickets.

### The constraint

At market rates the VP Product's mix costs more than the $3.6M. And the comp committee will approve an AI market range above the standard bands only if it applies to everyone doing the AI role at that level, transfers included. It won't approve a premium for new hires alone, because the 40 L5 engineers in product engineering would see it as paying strangers more for the same level.

### What a strong answer covers

- What each hiring and pay approach costs, and how long each takes to put a team in seats before the back-to-school release.
- How you level AI engineers on the existing ladder without inflating titles to close offers.
- What the VP Product gives up, and how you agree it.
- How you treat internal equity for the engineers who aren't joining the AI team.
- How you bring the CFO, HR and the comp committee, recruiting, product and the engineering managers along.
